### Formas que contienen $`a+bu`$

47. $`\int\frac{u\,du}{a+bu}=\frac1{b^2}(a+bu-a\ln|a+bu|)+C`$

01. $`\int\frac{u^2\,du}{a+bu}=\frac1{2b^3}+[(a+bu)^2-4a(a+bu)+2a^2\ln|a+bu|]+C`$

01. $`\int\frac{du}{u(a+bu)}=\frac1a\ln\left|\frac u{a+bu}\right|+C`$

01. $`\int\frac{du}{u^2(a+bu)}=-\frac1{au}+\frac b{a^2}\ln\left|\frac{a+bu}u\right|+C`$

01. $`\int\frac{u\,du}{(a+bu)^2}=\frac a{b^2(a+bu)}+\frac1{b^2}\ln|a+bu|+C`$

01. $`\int\frac{du}{u(a+bu)^2}=\frac1{a(a+bu)}-\frac1{a^2}\ln\left|\frac{a+bu}u\right|+C`$

01. $`\int\frac{u^2\,du}{(a+bu)^2}=\frac1{b^3}\left(a+bu-\frac{a^2}{a+bu}-2a\ln|a+bu|\right)+C`$

01. $`\int u\sqrt{a+bu}\,du=\frac2{15b^2}(3bu-2a)(a+bu)^{3/2}+C`$

01. $`\int\frac{u\,du}{\sqrt{a+bu}}=\frac2{3b^2}(bu-2a)\sqrt{a+bu}+C`$

01. $`\int\frac{u^2\,du}{\sqrt{a+bu}}=\frac2{15b^3}(8a^2+3b^2u^2-4abu)\sqrt{a+bu}+C`$

01. La fórmula se presenta en dos casos:

    $$
    \int\frac{du}{u\sqrt{a+bu}}=\begin{cases}
        \frac1{\sqrt a}\ln\left|\frac{\sqrt{a+bu}-\sqrt a}{\sqrt{a+bu}+\sqrt a}\right|+C&(a\gt 0)\cr
        \frac2{\sqrt{-a}}\tan^{-1}\sqrt{\frac{a+bu}{-a}}+C&(a\lt 0)
        \end{cases}
    $$

01. $`\int\frac{\sqrt{a+bu}}u\,du=2\sqrt{a+bu}+a\int\frac{du}{u\sqrt{a+bu}}`$

01. $`\int\frac{\sqrt{a+bu}}{u^2}\,du=-\frac{\sqrt{a+bu}}u+\frac b2\int\frac{du}{u\sqrt{a+bu}}`$

01. $`\int u^n\sqrt{a+bu}\,du=\frac{2u^n(a+bu)^{3/2}}{b(2n+3)}-\frac{2na}{b(2n+3)}\int\frac{u^{n-1}}{\sqrt{a+bu}}\,du`$

01. $`\int\frac{u^n\,du}{\sqrt{a+bu}}=\frac{2u^n\sqrt{a+bu}}{b(2n+1)}-\frac{2na}{b(2n+1)}\int\frac{u^{n-1}\,du}{\sqrt{a+bu}}`$

01. $`\int\frac{du}{u^n\sqrt{a+bu}}=-\frac{\sqrt{a+bu}}{a(n-1)u^{n-1}}-\frac{b(2n-3)}{2a(n-1)}\int\frac{du}{u^{n-1}\sqrt{a+bu}}`$
